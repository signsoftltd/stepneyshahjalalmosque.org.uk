For an accurate compass

  • Hold the phone flat, screen facing up.
  • Keep away from metal, magnets and electronics.
  • If the direction jumps, calibrate: move the phone in a figure of eight a few times.
  • Inside the masjid, follow the direction of the prayer rows.
